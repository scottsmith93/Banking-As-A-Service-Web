# Banking as a Service

### 2026-10-23 — Quieter Sage Wallet overview

The account identity is now Sage Wallet, with its logo beside the H1 rather than repeated in the recent table. The info notice sits right-aligned beside View all transactions. The overview Justified cells show completed invoice links or categories only; unfinished states are blank, while Bank movements retains action states. Shared source cells now show card name, cardholder and illustrative photo avatars; non-card activity never invents a cardholder. Headshots use external Random User demo images with a Carbon person-icon fallback and are not actual photos of the fictional account owners. Shared status pills are borderless with a 5% semantic tint and matching text; signed inflows and the money-in KPI use bold green figures with regular-weight signs.

## Purpose

This prototype explores how Sage can introduce Adyen-powered bank accounts and debit cards into the existing Banking experience. It focuses on the account list, account onboarding, the active-account dashboard, and debit-card management.

## Key decisions

- The account header prioritises New card and Transfer, with Add money and Statements in Carbon's keyboard-accessible three-dot ActionPopover. Money movement and statements retain their existing prototype-only scope.
- Two equal-width daily charts live inside the balance/KPI surface: Money movements uses grouped green/red bars, and Balance history uses the bright-blue closing-balance line. Each plot is 136px high with 24px total separation from the KPI row, its own labelled scale, and accessible daily descriptions. Carbon text/layout and token-styled native SVG compose this pattern because installed Sage Carbon exports no chart. Brighter chart-only fills use the Sage brand-green and modern red-soft tokens; Financial text retains its darker readable tokens. Gridlines are a faint token-derived tint. The charts share one daily dataset and date range through the demo API snapshot. CSS grid wraps them into a stack when their actual container cannot hold two readable charts; each plot independently measures its width and adapts date-tick density and localized axis gutters. Plot areas align even when headings/legends wrap. The money-movement axis starts at zero; the balance scale follows its data and its accessible description explains that it is scaled to show changes. Daily settlements/fees enrich the shared feed without replacing the ten newest records. The balance line is reconstructed from the final snapshot and completed movements; a real API should supply historic ledger balances, including holds.
- Each chart has one keyboard stop and an inspectable whole daily grid, including zero-activity days. Hovering or Left/Right/Home/End shows the same localized daily values in a white Carbon Tooltip with date, labelled values and right-aligned EUR amounts. Carbon supplies the portal, arrow, collision flipping and positioning; an SVG foreignObject anchors it to the actual data coordinate. The standalone Tooltip is the installed Carbon export (no __next__ equivalent); this chart-specific composition is a candidate for a future Carbon chart pattern. Popup content stays hoverable, Escape dismisses it, and a shared inspection owner prevents two chart popups competing when switching between pointer and keyboard. Custom chart focus matches Carbon's focus tokens; live status text announces keyboard values. The original full-data SVG descriptions remain available.
- Money-in/out KPIs and chart bins come from the same completed account transactions, with integer-cent arithmetic. Pending includes all outstanding account transactions, including those initiated before the chart month. Available balance is a separate API snapshot.
- The recent list remains capped at ten, including a completed customer sales-invoice payment. Its shop/customer Source, invoice-payment type and Sales-labelled evidence link share the same paid invoice record as Sales. Purchase links are labelled Purchase; incomplete overview evidence remains blank.
- Monthly debit card spend now uses Carbon FlatTable with the same flat headers, horizontal rules, 56px minimum rows and shared 32px photo avatars/name alignment as transactions. A row represents one issued card, including zero-spend cards, with a native SVG ID-1 thumbnail in its Mint/Midnight/Plum style, readable nickname and last four digits. Click or keyboard-activate a row to open that card's existing detail view. The bounded overview shows the five highest-spend active cards and a translated shown/total count; View all debit cards opens the complete card page.
- Card-spend totals use the shared card API-snapshot fixtures, not the bounded recent-transaction window. Per-card transaction counts, merchants and prior-period amounts are clearly marked demo data in `src/data/cardSpendSummaries.ts`. Comparison is month-to-date against the same elapsed period last month (1–23 October versus 1–23 September in this snapshot), with both date ranges and previous amounts visible. Integer-cent totals and one-decimal locale-aware percentages are calculated rather than hardcoded. Increase/decrease copy is neutral, not a judgement of business spending. Zero baselines and missing history never produce infinity or a fabricated percentage. A newly created card without a prior-period snapshot shows Comparison unavailable. The fixture cards belong only to the operating account; the pending account has an empty spend state.

- Existing legacy accounts remain visible alongside Sage bank accounts.
- The promotional banner pairs the Copilot mark with layered Sage card visuals and opens a viewport-sized, Sage-headed onboarding dialog with a 40px edge gutter.
- Completing the onboarding flow stages two demonstration accounts: one active and one pending review.
- The active account dashboard links to a debit-card view whose tiles draw each card at real ID-1 proportions in the light Sage Wallet styles (Mint, Midnight, Plum), with All, Issued, and Deactivated tabs. Search and Create debit card share the title row. Sage Wallet issues Mastercard only, so every card, transaction, and the issuing form use the Mastercard mark and there is no network choice. Card issuing (Create debit card on the cards page, New card on the overview) opens the Sage Wallet wizard at its card step: card type, cardholder (or add a new one), monthly limit, card style, and delivery address for physical cards, with a live preview.
- The prototype uses Carbon React and Sage design tokens, with the new composed UI kept in the `banking-baas` component group.

## Localization scope

The Sage Banking shell is available in English (`en-GB`), Spanish (`es-ES`), French (`fr-FR`), German (`de-DE`), and Portuguese (`pt-PT`). The provider-hosted onboarding surface intentionally stays in the exact English Figma copy so the external UI treatment remains faithful for stakeholder review.

## Known limitations

The transaction workspace and its Figma/competitor analysis are documented in [transactions/README.md](transactions/README.md). Open `/banking/bank-movements?account=sage-operating-account&view=all` to justify transactions, upload a document, or link an existing invoice from the EUR demo snapshot. The overview's ten newest transactions reuse the same column and cell definitions as Bank movements. Old per-account transaction URLs redirect here.

- Account creation, transfers, card issuing, and card detail actions are demonstration states only. Cardholders and restrictions are mock options. Issuing adds card metadata to shared in-memory state across Banking routes; refreshing resets it. Virtual cards are ready to use and physical cards appear as Ordered. The PIN is validated temporarily and is never retained in the demo card record; no shipment or issuing API request is made.
- Adyen onboarding is represented by a local prototype flow rather than a live provider integration.
- The approval state and pending account are local demonstration states rather than live provider responses.
- Dashboard transactions and cardholders are mock data.
- Network and wallet artwork is exported from the supplied Figma design and stored locally in `public/assets/banking-baas`; the panel makes no third-party image requests.
